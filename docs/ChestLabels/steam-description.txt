Name your chests. The name floats over the chest in the same style as grave quality, so you can tell your chests apart at a glance.

[h2]How It Works[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Walk up to a chest. Next to Open you'll see a second key, Label (the Work key, F by default).
[*]Press it and type a name, then press OK or Enter.
[*]The name now shows over that chest whenever you're nearby.
[*]Press Label again to change the name. Save it empty to remove it.
[/olist]

[h2]Good To Know[/h2]
[list]
[*]You can color a name with a color tag, such as <color=#c77dff>Relics</color>
[*]You can put one of the game's icons in a name with a sprite tag, such as <sprite name="gear">Iron
[*]Names are saved with your game and come back when you load.
[*]Taking a chest apart forgets its name.
[*]If you uninstall the mod, your chests stay as they are, just without names.
[/list]
