# Changelog

## 1.12.0

- Now runs on Timbn Core 2.0.0.
- Larry's offer of spare boards and of the lost Inquisitor's medallion is now a proper conversation you can accept or turn down.
- The boards offer shows up when you have fewer than two boards and no sawhorse or circular saw. The medallion offer only shows up if you lose the medallion before opening the tower prison bars.
- A Zombie Supplier with nowhere to put its item walks back to its station and shows the no storage icon, instead of standing still.

## 1.7.0

- Bait kept in a bag now counts when you start fishing. Before, the Keeper said there was no bait, and the Port, Forest and Sewers spots couldn't be fished at all.
- Anything you pick up, craft, buy, or take from a chest goes straight into the bag made for it while that bag has room, such as fish and bait into a fishing bag or seeds into a farming bag. Universal bags work as before.
- Config `Bug Fixes:BaitInBags` and `Tweaks:SortIntoBags` added.

## 1.6.0

- Updated for game version 1.007.
- Lumberjack, miner, clay and sand zombies no longer get stuck swinging forever after their stand is dismantled.
- Battle rewards that got knocked away come back to your feet when you sleep, including ones lost before you installed the mod.
- The chest window's move identical button now also takes matching items out of your bags.
- Larry now gives back a story item you lost before using it (the Inquisitor's medallion so far).
- The mod's text can now be translated.
- Removed the Port Area dock stairs fix and the garden well and study table fix, since the game fixes them now.

## 1.4.0

- Removed the zombie cooks losing ingredients fix, since 1.006 gives ovens room for every ingredient and fixes old ovens when you load.
- Removed the study table stopping partway fix, since the game now fixes it fully.
- The garden well and study table fix now only covers older wells and tables, since the game limits new ones itself.
- Config `Bug Fixes:OvenNoLostIngredients` and `Bug Fixes:StudyTableNoStuckCrafts` removed.

## 1.3.3

- The study table fix now covers more storage.
- Config `Bug Fixes:StudyTableScienceOnly` renamed to `Bug Fixes:SpecialStorageFilters`.
- Config `StudyTableScienceOnly` setting carries over to the new entry.
