# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Now runs on Timbn Core 2.2.0.
- The Mods window shows an About card for this mod, with a description and links to its pages, and lists it under Timbn Mods.
- Taking a parcel for a delivery shows the game's note for a new item.
- Petting the cat by the club walks you over and plays a petting animation.

## 1.0.0

- First release. The village and the port get a daily life.
- Herm, Linda, Larry and the astrologer follow daily routines, and Herm only trades during his opening hours.
- Villagers visit the graveyard each day, with more arriving as the town grows more grateful, and stroll its paths, graves, benches and church.
- The day after you bury a body, villagers hold a funeral for it.
- About once a week a neighbor asks you to cook a dish or carry a parcel across town, and pays its sale price.
- Translated into all eleven of the game's languages.
