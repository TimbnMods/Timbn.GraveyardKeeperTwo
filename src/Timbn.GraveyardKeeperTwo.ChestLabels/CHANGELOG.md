# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- A chest's name now shows under the Open and Label hints. The new LabelBelow setting turns that off to show it above them.
- The naming window has a color picker, copied from the character editor. The new Colors setting adds your own colors before the default ones.
- The naming window has a size for each chest, Small, Default, Large or Extra Large. The new DefaultSize setting sets how big Default is, as a percent of the game's text or as a font size, and the other sizes follow it.
- A chest keeps its name when a mod that moves buildings moves it, if that mod supports it.
- The Label hint shows the controller button when you play with a controller.

## 1.0.0

- First release. Press Label at a chest to give it a name that floats over it, in the same style as grave quality.
- Names can hold a color tag or one of the game's icons through a sprite tag.
- Names are saved with your game, and taking a chest apart loses its name.
