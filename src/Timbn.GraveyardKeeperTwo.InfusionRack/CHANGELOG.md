# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Now runs on Timbn Core 2.2.0.
- The Mods window shows an About card for this mod, with a description and links to its pages, and lists it under Timbn Mods.

## 1.0.0

- First release. Your zombies can earn tech points.
- A new Infusion Rack tech on the Anatomy tab, right after Body Places II.
- Distill hearts, guts and brains at the Distillation Cube into Red, Green and Blue Spirit Solutions, one per white skull.
- Build the Infusion Machine at the Morgue desk with two Infusion Stands, lay zombies in the stands and infuse them with a solution and a Blue Crystal.
- Infusions need skull mastery 4.
- Config `Infusion Rack:PointsPerSolution` added.
- Translated into all eleven of the game's languages.
