# Changelog

## 1.3.0

- Now runs on Timbn Core 2.0.0.
- Meditating ends by itself if you get tired.

## 1.2.0

- Sit by a campfire to pass the time faster while you slowly get energy back.
- Your insanity slowly goes down while you meditate, by up to 20 each time.
- Only the campfire at your home works by default. Turn off the HomeOnly setting to meditate at any campfire.
- You stay put while you meditate, and you can't start meditating during a talk.
